# Test Results

## Tested Phones

This section provides a list of phones and relevant information against which all the test cases mentioned in the [Test Case](./03-test-case.md) section were executed. The phone models were strategically chosen to cover a large part of the market shipment (market share) as well as the latest of the models at the time of testing. This ensures that there is a wide spread coverage. The selection was done on a per region basis across US, Asia Pacific (APA), Europe and China so as to provide data specific to a particular geography.

**NOTE**: The reported IOP test results reflect the outcome observed in the test environment and software versions used at the time of validation. A reported failure does not by itself indicate device or hardware incompatibility. In some cases, the result may be influenced by factors such as test application behavior, test firmware limitations, or device-specific conditions. Silicon Labs continues to review and improve the test infrastructure and supporting software, and readers are encouraged to refer to subsequent releases for the most up-to-date results.

### Summary of Tested Phone

The following table provides a high-level overview of the smartphones used for validation across iOS and Android platforms. The selected OS versions provide broad coverage. Testing included Apple iPhone models from the iPhone 11 through the iPhone 17 series, running iOS 26, the latest version available at the time of testing.
Android validation covered versions from Android 13 (released August 2022) through Android 16 (released June 2025), representing the range up to the most recent generally available Android release at the time of testing. Android devices from multiple global manufacturers were included to ensure diverse platform coverage.

| OS Platform |Number of Phones |Version Coverage |
|-|-|-|
| iOS |18 |OS Versions: 26.0, 26.0.1, 26.1 |
| Android |209 |OS Versions: 13, 14, 15, 16 |
